The Commons Concept for the second floor of the Dondorfdruckerei begins from a political premise: nine initiative groups — each working toward a climate-just, progressive Frankfurt — should share a floor that refuses hierarchy. The communal space (160 m²) is its spatial manifestation: a room that belongs to everyone and no one, adaptable between coworking, communal dining, exhibitions, lectures, and performances within the span of an afternoon.

The key spatial invention is the Mischnutz-Tischmodul — a modular table unit that can be rearranged to support any of these configurations. A four-part modular kitchen island with pass-through to the table zone makes communal cooking and eating an everyday act. The communal kitchen (18 m²) is designed for genuine gastronomy: dishwasher, filter system, two-ring induction hob, refrigerator, coffee machine.

My work at ORT3 spanned every phase of the project: from initial survey and as-built documentation through electrical planning, fire protection layouts, renovation drawings with change-of-use status, and the preparation of specification schedules. I produced the primary architectural drawings for the Schirn-owned floors independently, with sign-off from the project architect.
