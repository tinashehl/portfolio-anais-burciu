The Paradieshofplatz project is a study in the minimum viable intervention — and then its alternative. A vacant ground-floor commercial unit in the Paradieshof courtyard, Sachenhausen, was to be temporarily activated for the Uff die Gass street festival: a brief but public programme that required gastronomy, atmosphere, and quick reversibility.

Variante 1 — Minimaleingriff takes the existing building envelope at face value. The existing bar counter (with potential water connection) becomes a serving point, the existing electrical shaft provides current, and the storage and gastronomy functions are accommodated within the footprint of the existing facade mantle extended outward. The intervention is reversible within a day.

Variante 2 — Umnutzung proposes a more complete transformation: the interior is reconfigured, the existing window elements are renovated and opened to the courtyard, and the gastronomy kitchen is expanded. New water and electrical connections formalise what in Variante 1 is provisional. The building reads as a proper temporary bar-restaurant that belongs to the festival while remaining in dialogue with the quiet residential courtyard.

Both variants respond to the same analysis: the courtyard trees providing natural acoustic and visual privacy; the ELT shaft as latent infrastructure; the existing bar counter as spatial anchor. What changes is the degree of commitment — and the degree of transformation the landlord and festival organisers are prepared to authorise.

Design Variants

Variante 01

Minimaleingriff Bestand

Use of existing facade mantle as outer boundary. Existing bar counter and electrical connection exploited without new works. Gastronomy operates within and directly adjacent to the existing volume.

Reversibility: Full, within 24 hours.

Variante 02

Umnutzung des Innenraums

Window elements renovated and opened. Interior reconfigured. Gastronomy kitchen expanded with new water and electrical connections. Building reads as a permanent-temporary bar-restaurant.

Reversibility: Conditional; requires landlord approval.
